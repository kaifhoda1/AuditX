PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION POLICY
City Care Hospital Pvt Ltd
Version 1.0 | April 2026

1. DATA COLLECTION
We collect patient name, age, gender, contact number, email address, medical history,
diagnosis records, prescription data, and payment information during registration
and treatment. We also collect CCTV footage in hospital premises.

2. PURPOSE OF COLLECTION
Data is collected for patient treatment, billing, appointment scheduling,
and hospital administration purposes.

3. DATA STORAGE
All patient data is stored on our internal servers located in Mumbai, India.
We retain patient records for 7 years as per MCI guidelines.

4. DATA SHARING
We share patient data with referring doctors, diagnostic labs, and insurance companies
when required for treatment. We do not sell patient data to third parties.

5. SECURITY MEASURES
We use password protection on our systems. Staff are trained on data confidentiality.

6. PATIENT RIGHTS
Patients may request a copy of their records by visiting the hospital in person.

7. CONTACT
For any queries contact our front desk at citycare@hospital.com
